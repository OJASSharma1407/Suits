1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & MISSION STATEMENT
================================================================================

SUITS is an enterprise-grade AI-powered Court Intelligence, Case Analytics, and
Legal Research Platform purpose-built for the Indian Legal System. 

The Indian judiciary represents one of the largest and most complex legal ecosystems
in the world, operating under a common law framework governed by Article 141 of the
Constitution of India (stare decisis: Supreme Court rulings are binding law of the
land). With over 50 million pending cases across the Supreme Court of India, 25 High
Courts, and thousands of District and Subordinate Courts, legal practitioners face
an unprecedented cognitive and operational bottleneck.

SUITS bridges the gap between massive, fragmented judicial repositories and daily
advocacy workflows. It transforms raw court dockets, unstructured judgment texts,
and complex statutory shifts into actionable legal intelligence, publisher-grade
headnotes, predictive case analytics, and automated court-ready pleadings.

2. WHY SUITS IS NEEDED (COMPREHENSIVE PAIN POINT & INDUSTRY ANALYSIS)
================================================================================

2.1 PAIN POINT 1: DOCKET TRACKING & DATA FRAGMENTATION
  - Context: The eCourts portal houses case details across 3,000+ court complexes.
    Lawyers managing 50-100 active matters must manually input 16-character Case
    Number Record (CNR) strings repeatedly, solve CAPTCHAs, and navigate cryptic
    cause lists.
  - The SUITS Solution: Single-click CNR lookup, persistent caching, automated
    timeline generation with staggered hearing date extrapolation, and one-click
    case refreshing.

2.2 PAIN POINT 2: COMPREHENDING 100-PAGE JUDGMENTS UNDER TIME PRESSURE
  - Context: An advocate appearing before a High Court bench often has under 5
    minutes to distill a 70-page judgment cited unexpectedly by opposing counsel.
    Judgments intermingle counsel submissions, trial court factual records, obiter
    dicta, and operative holdings.
  - The SUITS Solution: The Headnote & Order Intelligence Engine performs Rhetorical
    Role Segmentation (RRS) using InLegalBERT to mathematically isolate the ratio
    decidendi from party arguments, producing SCC/AIR-style publisher headnotes with
    hierarchical catchwords, numbered held points, and plain-language summaries.

2.3 PAIN POINT 3: THE JULY 2024 STATUTORY TRANSITION CRISIS
  - Context: On 1 July 2024, the Parliament of India repealed and replaced the three
    foundational criminal law codes:
      1. Indian Penal Code, 1860 (IPC) -> Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)
      2. Code of Criminal Procedure, 1973 (CrPC) -> Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS)
      3. Indian Evidence Act, 1872 (IEA) -> Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 (BSA)
    Under Section 531 BNSS and Section 358 BNS, offences committed prior to 1 July 2024
    must be tried under the old IPC/CrPC, while offences committed thereafter fall
    under BNS/BNSS. Thousands of pleadings are currently being dismissed or returned
    with objections due to improper section references and failure to comply with
    new procedural mandates (e.g., mandatory videography under S. 105 BNSS).
  - The SUITS Solution: The Era Transition Analyzer detects the filing era, maps all
    cited sections across eras using a curated concordance database, issues procedural
    delta alerts, and generates court-ready transition clauses.

2.4 PAIN POINT 4: PRECEDENT SEARCH & THE BINDING AUTHORITY HIERARCHY
  - Context: Finding a precedent that is "on all fours" requires evaluating judicial
    hierarchy (Supreme Court 3-judge bench > 2-judge bench > High Court full bench >
    High Court division bench). Generic search engines fail to distinguish whether a
    case was followed, distinguished, or overruled.
  - The SUITS Solution: Hybrid RAG fuses Kanoon citation graphs with InLegalBERT dense
    embeddings, applies a 1.5x stare decisis multiplier for Supreme Court judgments,
    and constructs an interactive D3 citation network graph.

2.5 PAIN POINT 5: DRUDGERY OF OPPOSING PLEADING DRAFTING (ORDER VIII CPC)
  - Context: When served with a 40-page Plaint or Writ Petition, advocates must draft
    a Written Statement adhering to Order VIII Rules 3-5 CPC (mandating that every
    allegation not specifically denied is deemed to be admitted). Drafting 50+
    paragraph-by-paragraph denials and identifying legal bars consumes 6-10 hours.
  - The SUITS Solution: The Adversarial Defense Engine ingests the plaint, extracts
    numbered paragraphs via deterministic regex, flags statutory bars (Order VII
    Rule 11, Section 12A Commercial Courts Act, Limitation Act), and drafts a
    complete Written Statement ready for insertion into the built-in Legal Editor.

4. SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE & DESIGN PHILOSOPHY
================================================================================

SUITS is engineered with a modern, high-performance, two-tier decoupled architecture:
  1. Frontend: Single Page Application (SPA) built on React 19, TypeScript, and Vite.
  2. Backend: Asynchronous REST API built on Python 3.12, FastAPI, and Uvicorn.

CORE ARCHITECTURAL PRINCIPLES:
  1. Local-First AI & Dual-Mode Flexibility:
     - Cloud Mode: Uses Google Gemini 3.5 Flash for high-speed reasoning and
       OpenRouter for SSE streaming chat.
     - Local Mode: Uses Ollama (Qwen 2.5 7B) running entirely on localhost:11434.
       Zero bytes of sensitive case text leave the lawyer's machine.
     - Automatic Failover: When cloud API quotas (HTTP 429) are exhausted, the
       system prompts the user to switch seamlessly to the local model.
  2. Neuro-Symbolic Legal Processing:
     - Combines symbolic deterministic rules (regex parsing, statutory bar logic,
       era concordance tables) with neural AI models (InLegalBERT embeddings,
       Gemini generative deduction). This guarantees 100% legal accuracy on
       statutory section numbers while leveraging LLM reasoning for prose synthesis.
  3. Two-Tier Caching with Graceful Degradation:
     - Tier 1: In-memory Redis cache for high-frequency search and case queries.
     - Tier 2: Relational persistence (SQLite in dev, PostgreSQL in prod) for
       cached orders, headnotes, predictions, and analysis. If Redis is down,
       the system transparently falls back to the database without throwing errors.
  4. Stateless API Design:
     - All user state is conveyed via cryptographically signed JWT Bearer tokens
       (HS256). Backend instances can be horizontally scaled behind a load balancer.

6. BACKEND DEEP DIVE
================================================================================

6.1 ENTRY POINT & APPLICATION LIFECYCLE (backend/app/main.py)
-------------------------------------------------------------
The application entry point is implemented in backend/app/main.py (171 lines).
It establishes the FastAPI ASGI instance and orchestrates startup and shutdown
lifecycle hooks using the modern async contextmanager lifespan pattern.

STARTUP SEQUENCE:
  1. Structured Logging Initialization: Configures structlog with ISO-8601
     timestamps, log level filtering, and colored console formatting.
  2. Async Lifespan Execution:
     - Database Table Creation: Invokes `await init_db()`, executing
       `Base.metadata.create_all()`.
     - Zero-Downtime SQLite Column Migrations: Automatically inspects existing
       tables via `PRAGMA table_info()` and executes `ALTER TABLE ADD COLUMN` for
       newly added model attributes, avoiding manual Alembic migrations in dev.
     - Startup Cache Sanitization: Executes raw SQL queries to purge corrupted
       records:
         * `DELETE FROM cached_orders WHERE markdown LIKE '%PDF extraction failed%';`
         * `DELETE FROM cached_ai_analyses WHERE ai_json LIKE '%"confidence": 0%';`
  3. Middleware Registration (LIFO Order):
     - Adds CORSMiddleware, RequestLoggingMiddleware, RateLimitMiddleware, and
       AIProviderMiddleware.
  4. Router Registration: Registers all 12 API routers under the `/api` prefix.
  5. Global Exception Handling: Captures `SuitsBaseException` instances and maps
     them to structured JSON error responses with distinct `error_code` fields.

SHUTDOWN SEQUENCE:
  1. `await ecourts_client.close()`: Gracefully drains and closes the HTTP connection
     pool maintained by `httpx.AsyncClient`.
  2. `await cache_service.close()`: Closes the Redis connection pool.

6.4 DATABASE LAYER & ASYNCHRONOUS SESSION MANAGEMENT (backend/app/database/)
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
SUITS implements a modern async database access layer using SQLAlchemy 2.0:

SQLite Configuration (Development):
  - Driver: `aiosqlite`
  - Pool: `StaticPool` - ensures all async tasks within a process reuse the single
    underlying connection, eliminating "database is locked" errors in SQLite.
  - Connection PRAGMAs:
    * `PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL`: Enables Write-Ahead Logging, allowing concurrent
      readers alongside a single writer.
    * `PRAGMA busy_timeout=30000`: Sets a 30-second lock retry timeout.
    * `PRAGMA foreign_keys=ON`: Enforces relational integrity on delete/update.

PostgreSQL Configuration (Production):
  - Driver: `asyncpg`
  - Pool: `NullPool` (stateless connection pool suitable for serverless or container
    scaling) or `QueuePool` with `pool_size=10, max_overflow=20`.
  - `pool_pre_ping=True`: Verifies connection liveness before issuing queries.

Session Dependency (`get_db`):
  Each HTTP request receives an isolated `AsyncSession`. The dependency executes:
  ```python
  async with async_session_factory() as session:
      try:
          yield session
          await session.commit()
      except Exception:
          await session.rollback()
          raise
      finally:
          await session.close()
  ```

6.5 MIDDLEWARE STACK (LIFO EXECUTION CHAIN)
-------------------------------------------
Middleware is registered in `main.py` and executes in Last-In-First-Out order:

1. `AIProviderMiddleware`:
   - Inspects the `X-AI-Provider` HTTP header on inbound requests.
   - Sets `request.state.ai_provider = "ollama"` or `"cloud"`.
   - Allows services to dynamically select the inference client without tight
     coupling to HTTP headers.
2. `RateLimitMiddleware`:
   - Implements a distributed token-bucket algorithm using Redis.
   - Key: `ratelimit:{client_ip}`. Default: 300 requests per minute.
   - Graceful Degradation: If Redis is unavailable, the limiter logs a warning
     and allows the request to pass through without blocking the user.
3. `RequestLoggingMiddleware`:
   - Records method, path, status code, and latency in milliseconds (`elapsed_ms`).
   - Uses structlog for machine-readable JSON logging. Excludes `/api/health`.
4. `CORSMiddleware`:
   - Configured with regex to allow all local development origins (`localhost`, `127.0.0.1`).
   - Exposes `Content-Disposition` (for file downloads) and `X-AI-Provider`.

6.7 SERVICES LAYER - EXHAUSTIVE FUNCTIONAL BREAKDOWN (14 SERVICES)
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. `CaseService` (case_service.py):
   - Orchestrates case retrieval across eCourts and Indian Kanoon.
   - Implements `_is_criminal_case()` using regex scanning for criminal keywords:
     `bail`, `crl`, `fir`, `ipc`, `crpc`, `bns`, `bnss`, `bsa`, `ndps`, `pocso`,
     `nia`, `cbi`, `pmla`, `quashing`, `section 482`, `habeas corpus`.
   - Normalizes disparate date formats (e.g., `12-04-2023`, `12/04/2023`) to standard
     ISO-8601 (`2023-04-12`).
   - Generates synthetic staggered timeline entries if court docket records contain
     sparse hearing data.

2. `OrderService` (order_service.py):
   - Retrieves judgment HTML from Indian Kanoon and converts it to clean Markdown
     via `kanoon_formatter.py`.
   - Dispatches judgment text to Gemini to extract 60 structured fields covering:
     Parties, Counsel, Outcome, Ratio Decidendi, Obiter Dicta, Catchwords,
     Held Points, and Precedent Citator Table.

3. `PredictionService` (prediction_service.py):
   - Computes a deterministic SHA-256 hash of the case state:
     `hash = SHA256(decision_date + next_date + orders_count + timeline_count)`.
   - Determines matter taxonomy (`BAIL`, `WRIT`, `NI_138`, `CRIMINAL_APPEAL`,
     `CIVIL_APPEAL`, `INTERIM_APP`, `DEFAULT`) and legislative era.
   - Retrieves top 6 precedents via `SimilarCasesService`.
   - Employs `prediction_gemini_client` with high-thinking budget (24,576 tokens)
     to generate calibrated outcome probability distributions.
   - Caches predictions for 7 days, auto-invalidating if the state hash changes.

4. `HeadnoteService` (headnote_service.py):
   - Executes a two-stage Neuro-Symbolic extraction:
     * Stage 1: InLegalBERT Rhetorical Role Segmentation (RRS) filters out counsel
       arguments and retains judicial holding sentences.
     * Stage 2: Gemini synthesizes holdings into publisher-grade headnotes with
       hierarchical catchwords, numbered held points, and a citator matrix.

5. `SimilarCasesService` (similar_cases_service.py):
   - Implements Hybrid RAG combining:
     * Method 1: Kanoon direct citation graph links (`cites`, `citedby`).
     * Method 2: Dense vector similarity using Gemini `embedding-001`.
     * Method 3: InLegalBERT whitened embeddings reranking.
   - Merges results using Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) with a 1.5x multiplier for
     Supreme Court precedents.

6. `CitationService` (citation_service.py):
   - Traverses Kanoon citation networks to construct nodes and edges.
   - Classifies nodes by tier: `sc` (gold), `hc` (blue), `tribunal`, `district`,
     and `statute` (grey).
   - Generates D3 force-directed layout compatible graph JSON.

7. `EraTransitionService` (era_transition_service.py):
   - Compares case filing dates against the 1 July 2024 boundary.
   - Classifies cases into: `NEW_BNS_ERA`, `LEGACY_IPC_ERA`, or `HYBRID_TRANSITION_ERA`.
   - Cross-references 100+ statute pairs in `criminal_statutes_concordance.py`.
   - Generates procedural alerts for S. 105 (videography), S. 173(3) (preliminary
     inquiry), S. 187 (custody split), and S. 479 (undertrial bail).

8. `CounterPleadingService` (counter_pleading_service.py):
   - Core engine for the Adversarial Defense Engine (Order VIII CPC).
   - Chunks plaints using regex `PARA_PATTERN` into numbered paragraphs.
   - Classifies paragraphs into `FORMAL`, `SUBSTANTIVE`, `PRAYER`, `BOILERPLATE`.
   - Detects statutory bars: Section 12A Commercial Courts Act (mediation non-compliance),
     Limitation Act 1963, Order VII Rule 11 CPC, jurisdiction defects.
   - Generates hybrid traversals: boilerplate templates for formal paras, Gemini
     synthesis for substantive allegations.

9. `DocumentProcessor` (document_processor.py):
   - Validates user uploads up to 25MB (PDF, DOCX, DOC, TXT, MD, PNG, JPG, TIFF).
   - Extraction pipeline: `pdfplumber` for digital PDFs, `python-docx` for Word,
     `pytesseract` OCR for scanned images/PDFs.
   - Chunks text into 800-character segments with 150-character overlap.
   - Generates embeddings via Gemini `embedding-001`.

10. `AnalyticsService` (analytics_service.py):
    - Scans cached case dockets using regex for 20+ Canonical Indian Acts.
    - Aggregates top cited acts, court tier distributions, and weekly trends.
    - Generates 365-day activity heatmap data from `CaseViewHistory`.

11. `ChatService` (chat_service.py):
    - Manages persistent conversation threads in SQLite/PostgreSQL.
    - Injects case context (parties, acts, orders) into the system prompt.
    - Streams tokens in real-time via Server-Sent Events (SSE) using OpenRouter.

12. `HistoryService` (history_service.py):
    - Records case views with deduplication per user per case per calendar day.

13. `BookmarkService` (bookmark_service.py):
    - Manages user case bookmarks with metadata (title, court, date).

14. `FileService` (file_service.py):
    - Manages user-saved Indian Kanoon documents in the Research Vault.

7.5 HTTP CLIENT, INTERCEPTOR STATE MACHINE & TOKEN REFRESH (lib/axios.ts)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Base Axios instance configured with `VITE_API_URL || http://localhost:8000/api`.

Request Interceptor:
  - Attaches `Authorization: Bearer {token}` from `localStorage`.
  - Reads `useAIModeStore.getEffectiveProvider()`. If `'local'`, attaches
    `X-AI-Provider: ollama`.

Response Interceptor (Error Handling & State Machine):
  - Token Refresh (HTTP 401):
    If a request fails with 401 and has not been retried:
    1. Pauses outbound requests.
    2. Sends `POST /api/auth/refresh` with `refresh_token`.
    3. On success: Updates tokens in `localStorage` and replays original request.
    4. On failure: Purges tokens and dispatches `window.dispatchEvent(new Event("auth:logout"))`.
  - AI Limit Interception (HTTP 429 / `ONLINE_AI_LIMIT_REACHED`):
    1. Captures the failed request closure as `pendingRetry`.
    2. Opens `SwitchToOllamaModal`.
    3. When user approves: Sets mode to `'local'` and replays `pendingRetry()`.

9. AI PIPELINE ARCHITECTURE & SPECIALIZED MODELS
================================================================================

9.1 AI ORCHESTRATOR & MULTI-PROVIDER STRATEGY
---------------------------------------------
The `AIOrchestrator` centralizes all model routing decisions. Services request
inference from the orchestrator without hardcoding provider logic. The orchestrator
evaluates:
  1. Header Context (`request.state.ai_provider`): If set to `'ollama'`, routes
     immediately to `OllamaClient`.
  2. Cloud Tier Availability: Dispatches to `GeminiClient`. If Gemini reports quota
     exhaustion (HTTP 429), fails over to `GroqClient`.
  3. Task Specificity: Dedicated tasks like Outcome Prediction and Headnote Synthesis
     are routed to `PredictionGeminiClient` with a dedicated thinking budget.

9.2 LAW-AI/INLEGALBERT - DOMAIN-SPECIFIC LEGAL NLP ENGINE
---------------------------------------------------------
Generic BERT models suffer from anisotropy (representations collapse into a narrow
cone) and lack comprehension of legal concepts (e.g., confusing "appeal dismissed"
with "appeal allowed"). SUITS integrates `law-ai/InLegalBERT`, a transformer model
pre-trained on 5.4 million Indian Supreme Court and High Court judgments.

Key Capabilities:
  - Understands Indian legal terminology: *ratio decidendi*, *res judicata*,
    *locus standi*, *ad-interim injunction*, *habeas corpus*.
  - Performs sentence-level Rhetorical Role classification.
  - Computes semantic similarity between fact patterns and precedents.

9.3 MATHEMATICAL FORMULATIONS
-----------------------------

1. Whitening Transformation for Legal Embeddings:
   To eliminate representation collapse and ensure isotropic vector distribution,
   InLegalBERT embeddings undergo second-order whitening:
   
     v_whitened = (v_raw - mu) * W
   
   Where:
     - mu is the precomputed mean vector (dim: 768).
     - W is the whitening transformation matrix (dim: 768 x 768) derived from
       Singular Value Decomposition (SVD) of the covariance matrix:
       Sigma = U * Lambda * U^T  =>  W = U * Lambda^(-1/2).
   Whitened vectors are L2-normalized, making cosine similarity mathematically
   equivalent to Euclidean distance on a unit hypersphere.

2. Reciprocal Rank Fusion (RRF) with Stare Decisis Authority Multiplier:
   When ranking precedents, the Hybrid RAG engine merges rank lists from Citation
   Graph (r_cit), Dense Vector Search (r_vec), and InLegalBERT (r_bert):
   
     RRF_Score(d) = Alpha(d) * SUM_{m in M} [ 1 / (k + r_m(d)) ]
   
   Where:
     - k is a smoothing constant (default: 60).
     - M = {CitationGraph, DenseVector, InLegalBERT}.
     - Alpha(d) is the Stare Decisis Multiplier:
       * Alpha = 1.5 if d is a Supreme Court of India judgment (Article 141 binding law).
       * Alpha = 1.0 if d is a High Court judgment.

9.4 RHETORICAL ROLE SEGMENTATION (RRS)
--------------------------------------
Headnote extraction employs anchor-based Rhetorical Role Segmentation:
  - Canonical holding anchors (_RATIO_ANCHORS): "We hold that...", "The question of
    law is answered in the affirmative...", "In view of the settled position..."
  - Argument anchors (_ARGUMENT_ANCHORS): "The learned counsel contended...",
    "It was submitted on behalf of the appellant..."
  - Each judgment sentence is embedded via InLegalBERT and projected against the
    anchor centroids. Sentences with minimum distance to `_ARGUMENT_ANCHORS` are
    pruned, preventing counsel arguments from being misidentified as court holdings.

9.5 ERA STATUTORY GUIDE & PROMPT ENFORCEMENTS
---------------------------------------------
All generative prompts for prediction, headnote synthesis, and chat inject the
`ERA_STATUTORY_GUIDE` system directive:
  - If filing date >= 2024-07-01: Prohibits citing S. 439 CrPC (must cite S. 483 BNSS),
    S. 438 CrPC (must cite S. 482 BNSS), S. 482 CrPC (must cite S. 528 BNSS),
    S. 420 IPC (must cite S. 318(4) BNS), S. 65B Evidence Act (must cite S. 63 BSA).
  - If filing date < 2024-07-01: Enforces legacy statutory references.

12. AUTHENTICATION, AUTHORIZATION & SECURITY ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

12.1 EMAIL/PASSWORD REGISTRATION & RESEND OTP VERIFICATION FLOW
--------------------------------------------------------------
  1. Registration (`POST /api/auth/register`):
     - Client submits `email`, `password`, `full_name`.
     - Validates email syntax via `email-validator`.
     - Hashes password using `passlib.context.CryptContext` with bcrypt (12 rounds).
     - Inserts record into `users` with `is_verified=False`.
     - Generates cryptographically secure 6-digit OTP via `secrets.randbelow(900000) + 100000`.
     - Stores OTP in `email_verification` table with a 10-minute expiration.
     - Dispatches HTML verification email via Resend API (`api.resend.com`).
  2. OTP Verification (`POST /api/auth/verify-otp`):
     - Client submits `email` and `otp_code`.
     - Queries `email_verification` where `otp_code == input`, `used == False`,
       and `expires_at > UTC now`.
     - On match: Sets `used=True`, updates `users.is_verified=True`.
     - Returns JWT access and refresh token pair.

12.2 GOOGLE OAUTH2 CREDENTIAL VERIFICATION FLOW
-----------------------------------------------
  1. Frontend Integration:
     - `@react-oauth/google` renders the Google One-Tap or Sign-In button.
     - Google client SDK returns an ID token credential.
  2. Backend Verification (`POST /api/auth/google-auth`):
     - Receives `{ credential: id_token }`.
     - Invokes `google.oauth2.id_token.verify_oauth2_token(credential, requests.Request(), GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID)`.
     - Extracts verified `email`, `name`, `sub` (Google user ID), and `picture`.
     - Upserts `users` record: matches by email, sets `auth_provider='google'`,
       `google_id=sub`, `avatar_url=picture`, `is_verified=True`.
     - Generates and returns SUITS JWT token pair.

12.3 DUAL-SECRET JWT ARCHITECTURE & TOKEN REFRESH FLOW
------------------------------------------------------
To maximize security, SUITS isolates access token signing from refresh token signing:
  - Access Token:
    * Signed with `JWT_SECRET` using `HS256`.
    * Payload: `{ sub: user_id, email: email, exp: now + 30 minutes, type: 'access' }`.
  - Refresh Token:
    * Signed with `JWT_REFRESH_SECRET` using `HS256`.
    * Payload: `{ sub: user_id, exp: now + 7 days, type: 'refresh' }`.
  - Token Refresh Endpoint (`POST /api/auth/refresh`):
    * Requires valid, unexpired refresh token.
    * Verifies `type == 'refresh'`.
    * Issues a fresh access token and rotatable refresh token.

12.4 DATA CONFIDENTIALITY, PRIVILEGE & CLIENT-SIDE PRIVACY
----------------------------------------------------------
Under Section 126 of the Indian Evidence Act (and Section 132 of BSA), communications
between an advocate and their client are privileged and confidential.
  - Local AI Execution: Lawyers handling highly sensitive criminal, corporate, or
    matrimonial matters can toggle AI Mode to "Local" (Ollama Qwen 7B). All
    paragraph traversals, case summarizations, and chat inquiries run entirely in
    local memory on `localhost:11434`. Zero prompt tokens are sent over the internet.
  - Ephemeral Drafts: Unsaved text pasted into the Adversarial Defense Engine or
    Legal Editor remains strictly in the client's browser memory until explicitly saved.

17. DEPLOYMENT, INFRASTRUCTURE & DEVOPS ARCHITECTURE
================================================================================

17.1 LOCAL DEVELOPMENT WORKFLOW
  - Prerequisites: Python 3.12+, Node.js 20+, Docker (for Redis).
  - Launching:
    ```powershell
    # Run the automated launch script
    .\start-all.ps1
    ```
    This script concurrently boots:
    1. FastAPI Backend on `http://localhost:8000` via Uvicorn reload.
    2. React Frontend on `http://localhost:5173` via Vite dev server.
    3. Redis 7 container on port 6379 via `docker-compose up -d`.

17.2 PRODUCTION ARCHITECTURE & ENVIRONMENT CONFIGURATION
  - Containerization: Backend and Frontend are packaged into multi-stage Dockerfiles.
  - Reverse Proxy (Nginx): Terminates SSL/TLS, routes `/api/*` to Uvicorn, and
    serves static frontend assets with aggressive gzip/brotli compression.
  - Production Environment Variables:
    * `DATABASE_URL`: `postgresql+asyncpg://user:pass@db:5432/suits`
    * `REDIS_URL`: `redis://redis:6379/0`
    * `ENVIRONMENT`: `production` (disables `/docs` and `/redoc` Swagger endpoints)
    * `CORS_ORIGINS`: `https://suits.yourdomain.com`

17.3 HORIZONTAL SCALING & BOTTLENECK MITIGATION
  - Stateless Web Tier: FastAPI backend instances do not maintain in-process session
    state. They scale horizontally behind AWS ALB, GCP Cloud Load Balancing, or Nginx.
  - Database Connection Pooling: `asyncpg` with `NullPool` ensures that scaling to
    dozens of container instances does not exhaust PostgreSQL connection limits.
  - InLegalBERT Memory Footprint: The PyTorch InLegalBERT model runs in-process,
    consuming ~450MB of RAM per worker with CPU threads capped at 2 to prevent CPU
    starvation.

18. TROUBLESHOOTING & OPERATIONAL RUNBOOK
================================================================================

1. Problem: "database is locked" error in development.
   - Cause: SQLite does not support concurrent write transactions across multiple
     processes or non-pooled connections.
   - Fix: Ensure `DATABASE_URL` uses `sqlite+aiosqlite:///./suits.db` with `StaticPool`
     and `PRAGMA journal_mode=WAL` (configured automatically in `session.py`).

2. Problem: Cloud AI calls fail with HTTP 429 (`ONLINE_AI_LIMIT_REACHED`).
   - Cause: Gemini or OpenRouter daily/minute API rate limits exceeded.
   - Automatic Mitigation: The frontend automatically displays `SwitchToOllamaModal`.
     Clicking "Switch to Local AI" redirects inference to the local Ollama daemon.

3. Problem: Ollama local model reports "Model not found".
   - Fix: Ensure Ollama is running (`ollama serve`) and pull the required model:
     ```bash
     ollama run qwen2.5:7b
     ```

4. Problem: PDF OCR extraction fails or returns empty text.
   - Cause: Tesseract OCR binary is not installed on the system PATH.
   - Fix: Install Tesseract (`winget install UB-Mannheim.TesseractOCR` on Windows or
     `apt-get install tesseract-ocr` on Linux) and restart the backend.
